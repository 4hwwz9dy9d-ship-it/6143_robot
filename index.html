        RULE-DRIVEN SIMULATION

        Pedestrian crossing

        Places a simulated person on the active aisle. The safety layer slows the robot, then A* may replan.

        Shopping cart blocking

        A cart occupies part of a corridor. The robot slows and takes a feasible side path when available.

        Aisle closure

        Closes a central cross aisle. The graph removes that edge and A* recomputes the leg.

        Robot overload

        Adds three 6 kg cases to the cart (18 kg). The 12 kg capacity check blocks departure.

        Shelf congestion

        Switches to the weekday peak crowd sample: higher time and risk costs change the chosen corridor.

        Navigation failure

        Injects a localization fault. The robot safe-stops until an operator explicitly recovers it.

        Simulation boundary: obstacle positions and reactions are generated by explicit rules in JavaScript. There is no trained vision classifier, no real-time inventory feed and no physical robot.           RESEARCH & METHODOLOGY

          What is real, what is calculated, what is simulated

          English interface terms with Chinese research explanations, as required for the course presentation.

          TRACEABLE
          REAL DATA

          Instacart cleaned CSV: 49,688 products, 3,421,108 orders, 1,384,617 train order-product lines, 131,209 train baskets. Product names and categories are unmodified.

          CALCULATED RESULTS

          Apriori support / confidence / lift and K-means clusters, computed offline by scripts/build_data.py. Route order and segment costs are computed in-browser by Held–Karp and A*.

          SIMULATED DATA

          Shelf coordinates, simulated stock, crowd samples, robot position, obstacle events, battery and travel time. Clearly marked Simulation in the interface.

          ASSUMPTIONS

          1 USD = ¥7 teaching rate, ¥30 minimum order, 12 kg capacity, 20 units per shelf, fixed robot speed and braking distance. No physical calibration.

          研究背景 / RESEARCH BACKGROUND

          从线上购物篮到店内机器人

          项目使用 Instacart 清洗数据集。商品名称、部门与真实 train 购物篮共购关系来自原始数据；研究问题是：能否由真实订单结构为店内自主移动机器人生成一个可解释、可复核的取货导航演示？

          算法说明 / ALGORITHM EXPLANATION

          Held–Karp + A*

          Held–Karp 对订单中的不同货架做状态压缩动态规划，最小化入口到结账的总路径代价；超过 12 个货架时退化为最近代价启发式，并在界面明确标注“不保证全局最优”。A* 在由过道节点构成的图上逐段搜索，边代价 = 距离 + 0.45 × 预计通行时间 + 1.3 × 风险，其中时间和风险来自教学客流样本。3D 场景与 2D 图使用同一份 storeData.js 坐标。

          数据来源 / DATA SOURCE

          Instacart cleaned & enriched CSV

          输入为五份已清洗 CSV：products_enriched_clean.csv、orders_clean.csv、order_products_train_clean.csv、aisles_clean.csv、departments_clean.csv。价格、重量与热量为数据集中已有的估算字段，并非本演示实测。

          研究方法 / METHODOLOGY

          离线挖掘 + 在线规划

          • Apriori：仅对购买频次前 600 件商品挖掘 1 项与 2 项频繁集，最小共购计数 35；输出真实 support、confidence、lift。
          • K-means：4,000 件高频食品，特征为对数变换后的估算属性与真实频次、复购比例及 aisle / department one-hot；肘部法选 K，轮廓系数仅作参考。
          • 路径：订单驱动 Held–Karp 顺序，A* 逐段规划，机器人沿实际路径点移动并更新任务。

          模拟假设 / SIMULATION ASSUMPTIONS

          明确边界

          • 店内客流人数、障碍物位置与通行时间为教学样本，不是传感器测量。
          • 机器人宽约 0.9 m、速度 22–26 地图单位/秒、制动距离约 2 单位为演示参数，未做实物校准。
          • 冷链、重箱、海鲜和肉类需要员工协助，网页未模拟真实抓取或条码识别。
          • 订单队列只保存在当前浏览器会话，刷新后清空。

          数据局限 / DATA LIMITATIONS

          不能得出的结论

          • Instacart 下单时段不等于店内货架客流，不能作为真实通行时间标签。
          • 共购关系不代表因果，也未做推荐增购效果的随机对照实验。
          • 清洗前原始异常删除量无法从已清洗文件反推，网页不虚构该数字。
          • 无真实传感器标注集，因此没有训练或验证视觉分类 / 通行时间回归模型。
